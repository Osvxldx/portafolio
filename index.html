Agentic AI Hackathon 2026, Salesforce

Prometheus, an AI sales agent

Sales executives at a financial firm were spending most of their day on admin work. Prometheus is an agent inside Salesforce that they talk to in plain language: it lists today's follow-ups, updates deals, builds the quarterly forecast and recommends products using company knowledge articles. Every action it takes is logged so people can see what changed and why.

Stack: Agentforce, Flows, RAG with Knowledge, Apex, Lightning Web Components. Scored 82.1 out of 100.

The story behind my smile

People always tell me I'm positive. Want to know why?

There was a season in my life when everything felt heavy, and I felt like no one really understood me. Some days, simply keeping going was the hardest thing I did.

I made it through, and I'm deeply grateful for that. That's where my smile comes from. Not from never being sad, but from knowing how it feels to be unseen.

So now I try to be the person I needed back then: I listen, I share, and I help whenever I can. Every day is my way of saying thank you to life.

That's why I believe it with my whole heart: you matter.
